# 📝 Changelog V1.3:

-  📱 **New "This Device" feature!** Choose from 4 profiles to override the phone's system performance, ranging from ***Efficient*** to ***Extreme.***

-  📴  **New "Screen Off" feature!** Choose from **3 new presets** when your phone is idling: ***Deep Sleep***, ***Light Sleep*** and ***Active Sleep!***

- **Supports all 118 CPUs!**

- ⚡ Reduced the rendering cost of **Interactive bubble animations** and **Miuix.**

- 🛠️ Reworked and added **new cool animations** across ***miuix and m3e!***

- ✨ **Reworked scrolling animations to be smoother.**

- ▶️ Added a **"Launch App"** and **"Create Home screen shortcut"** buttons.

- 📂 **Improved in-app ***logging features!*****
